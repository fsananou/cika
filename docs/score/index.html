Document A · Methodology

How CIKA scores a member.

The CIKA credit score is built from five additive pillars that sum to a maximum of 100 points. Each pillar reflects a behavioural or structural dimension of a member's standing in their savings group.

Default rule

Members who miss N consecutive meetings after receiving the pot have their full score forced to zero. The streak threshold is configurable per simulation.
